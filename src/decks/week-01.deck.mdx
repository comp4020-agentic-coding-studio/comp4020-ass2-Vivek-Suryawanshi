# Why is the night sky dark?

---

Look up on a clear night.

Most of the sky is black.

---

## Imagine a universe that is

- infinite
- eternal
- static
- stars, spread evenly, everywhere

---

{/* _class: banner */}

## Look in any direction

```
· · ★ · · · ★ ·
★ · · ★ · · · ·
    ·  ↑  ·  ★
      you
```

Follow that line far enough. It ends on a star.

---

## So every direction should end on something bright.

The whole sky should glow.

It does not.

---

{/* _class: impact */}

So the darkness is evidence.

---

## What has to give

Not eternal. Not static.

It has a history — and it is expanding.

---

## Finite age isn't the whole story

- stars don't shine forever
- expansion stretches and reddens light
- matter isn't spread perfectly evenly

---

{/* _class: quote */}

The dark between the stars is doing work.
