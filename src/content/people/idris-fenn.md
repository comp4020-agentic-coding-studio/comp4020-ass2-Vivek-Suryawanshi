Idris Fenn runs the four sessions where the lectures stop being enough on
their own. That means plotting real CMB data against a blackbody curve,
counting microstates by hand, redoing the Sun's lifetime calculation, and
working out what degeneracy pressure can and cannot hold up. Nothing in a
session is a demonstration — you leave having produced the number or the
count yourself, not having watched someone else produce it.
