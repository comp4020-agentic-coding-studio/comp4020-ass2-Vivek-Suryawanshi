Marisol Quaye convenes SLOP1849 and gives all twelve lectures. She built the
chain the course follows: each week ends on a question it cannot yet answer,
and the next week has to start there. In the School of Continuous
Improvement, that is the whole design brief — not a syllabus to cover, but a
question worth being stuck on until the following week gives you the tools
to move past it.
