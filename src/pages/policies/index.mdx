# Policies and support

SLOP1849 is taught in the School of Continuous Improvement.

## Use of AI

Using an AI tool to help build an explanation is permitted. It does not do the
marked work for you. An AI-generated explanation can sound complete and still
be confidently wrong, or quietly leave something out — finding those
omissions, oversimplifications and errors is your job, not the tool's. Where
an initial explanation came from is not what gets marked here. The judgement
you show about it is.

## Sources

Cite where a number, a claim, or a dataset came from. If a piece of work uses
a real measurement — a spectrum, a dated event, a physical constant — name the
source and give enough detail that someone else could find the same figure.
Be honest about the difference between an explanation you worked through
yourself and one you read or were given. Neither is a problem by itself;
presenting one as the other is.

## Mathematics

No formal mathematics is assumed before Week 7. Weeks 1 to 6 work with
numbers, estimates, graphs and comparisons, and nothing beyond that. Week 7 is
where a question stops being settleable by argument alone, and the course
starts expecting calculation instead. From Week 7 onward, show your working —
an estimate with no arithmetic behind it is not an answer.
